# Research Brief

## Core question
Chinese-founder (ethnic Chinese, or China-born US immigrant/returnee, broad definition) US/Silicon Valley tech startups founded 2012-2024 that achieved notable success (unicorn / large exit / large funding round). AI/ML prioritized, also 2012-2016 mobile-internet wave.

## Per-company fields to verify
1. Company name + main domain
2. Founder name(s) + Chinese/returnee background + evidence
3. Founding year
4. Major funding rounds: lead investor per round + approx date
5. Whether Chinese/China-linked funds appear on cap table (GGV/Qiming, HongShan/Sequoia China, ZhenFund, Sinovation, Hillhouse, Tencent, Source Code, IDG, GSR, Hua Chuang, DHVC, BAI, Morningside, Gaorong, Amino, Matrix/Jingwei, 5Y/Wuyuan). Yes/No/Unknown.

## Candidates (must verify)
Scale AI, Zoom, DoorDash, Nuro, Pony.ai, Notion, Pika, HeyGen, Fireworks AI, Together AI, Imbue, World Labs, Etched, Confluent, Wish/ContextLogic, Lime, Perplexity, Cresta, AutoX, Standard AI/Standard Cognition, Moveworks.

## Success criteria
Each company has: founder background w/ evidence, founding year, at least the major rounds with leads, and a clear Yes/No/Unknown on Chinese fund presence. Flag unverified.

## Out of scope
Companies with no notable Chinese-founder link; exhaustive every-round detail where unavailable.
